Method

How the engine works, what it assumes, and what is still to build.

1

Sense

Bookings, searches, sold-out searches (unmet demand), look-to-book, cancellations, plus events, visas, airfares and risk news.

2

Forecast

Demand for each product-week as 600 scenarios, with regional shocks that hit every product in a region together.

3

Decide

A greedy allocator adds stock in steps where expected extra gross profit per rupee is highest, inside the cash budget and the write-off cap.

4

Watch

The release radar compares pickup with forecast and sets how many allotment units to keep at T-14.

Assumptions

Not built yet (Round 2): forecasts trained on real booking and search logs, one contract per hotel season with week-level allotment sizes, a stochastic optimiser to compare against the greedy one, and live pickup feeds for the radar.
