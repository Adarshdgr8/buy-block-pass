Buy, Block or Pass

Inventory engine prototype · TBO VoyageHack 4.0 · Team IIM Lucknow
Synthetic data. Every figure is illustrative.

For every hotel-week and flight-week in Summer 2027 (April to June), the engine decides whether TBO should Buy (prepay rooms or series seats), Block (hold an allotment it can release 14 days out) or Pass (stay on free sale). It weighs 600 demand scenarios per week, including regional shocks, and never lets the bad-quarter write-off go past your limit. The test market here: 10 destinations, 40 hotels and 10 flight routes from India.

Same budget, two ways to spend it

Our engine vs the intuitive approach: prepay 60% of forecast demand in the hottest, most sold-out weeks until the budget runs out. Extra gross profit is measured against staying on free sale.

How it works

1 · Sense

Read demand TBO already sees

Bookings, searches, sold-out searches (unmet demand), look-to-book by market, cancellations, plus events, visas, airfares and risk news.

2 · Forecast

Ranges, not one number

Demand for each product-week as a spread of outcomes, with regional shocks that hit all of a region's products at once.

3 · Decide

Buy, Block or Pass

Adds inventory step by step where it earns the most per rupee, inside the cash budget and the write-off cap. Sets the sign-by date and agent price.

4 · Watch

Release radar

Tracks pickup after signing and flags how many allotment rooms to keep or hand back before each release date.

Assumptions and limits (please read)
ItemValue usedBasis
DataFully synthetic: 600 product-weeks, 600 scenarios each, fixed random seedNo TBO data used. Built to show the method, not to forecast TBO's results.
Free-sale margin8% hotels, 1.2% airHotel take rates of ~8% in TBO's Q1 FY27 shareholder letter; air gross profit guided at 1-1.2% on the Q2 FY27 call.
Prepay discount10-18% hotels, 8-14% series seats, smaller in peak weeksPublic ranges for hard allotments (10-25% below rack). Hotels discount least on dates they expect to fill.
Allotment rate4-8% below free sale, ±5 pts with seasonThe contracted rate is fixed; free-sale prices rise in peak weeks and fall in low season.
Release and attritionRelease at T-14; must keep at least 30% (hotels) / 40% (seats)Common release structures are 30/21/14/7 days; attrition clauses vary.
Unsold prepaid stockRecover 30% (hotels) / 15% (seats) through last-minute salesAssumption. Change it in engine.js to test sensitivity.
Saving passed to agents60%Own stock must undercut other wholesalers selling the same hotel.
Sold-out demandHalf is truly lost; the other half books an alternative on TBOAssumption. Only the truly lost half counts as upside.
Shocks (per quarter)Europe 15% chance, demand ×0.50 · Gulf 12%, ×0.55 · APAC 6%, ×0.75Set to echo the October 2026 Saudi and Europe cancellations TBO reported.
Contract cost₹15k per hotel-week, ₹40k per route-weekContracting, loading and reconciliation effort.
