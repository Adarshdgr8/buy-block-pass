02 · The trap

Buying rooms early helps, if you buy the right weeks

Hotels give the deepest prepay discounts on weeks they struggle to fill and the smallest on weeks they know will sell out. Prepay the hottest weeks and TBO pays close to full price for rooms it must then sell, whatever happens.

In October 2026, Saudi airport attacks and riots in Europe set off waves of cancellations. Rooms prepaid for weeks like those turn into write-offs.

03 · The options

There are three ways to hold a room

The question for each hotel-week and flight-week is which contract to sign, and for how many rooms or seats.

Buy

Prepay

The biggest discount, about 10-18%. TBO owns every room, sold or not.

Fits steady weeks and low seasons, when hotels discount hard.

Block

Allotment

A smaller discount and a 20% deposit. Fourteen days before arrival, TBO keeps what will sell and hands the rest back.

Fits busy weeks and markets prone to shocks.

Pass

Free sale

No commitment and the normal margin, so TBO risks nothing and earns nothing extra.

Fits weeks where demand fades or the upside can’t cover the risk.

04 · The engine

How the engine makes the call

  1. 1

    Sense

    Reads what TBO already sees: bookings, searches, sold-out searches, look-to-book, cancellations, plus events, visas and risk news.

  2. 2

    Forecast

    Turns each hotel-week into 600 possible outcomes, with regional shocks that hit a whole region at once.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Adds stock one step at a time where it earns most per rupee, and stops before the bad-quarter write-off crosses the cap.

  4. 4

    Watch

    After signing, tracks pickup and says how many allotment rooms to hand back before each release date.
